# Privacy Policy for NanoTV

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

NanoTV ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a parent-managed Android TV media organization utility designed for parents and legal guardians (18+) to curate public online video shelves for household television viewing.

## 1. YouTube API Services & Third-Party Policies

NanoTV uses **YouTube API Services** to retrieve public metadata for channels, playlists, and videos selected by the user and plays videos via the official YouTube IFrame Player API.

- By using NanoTV, you agree to be bound by the **YouTube Terms of Service**: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
- Information processed by Google and YouTube during embedded video playback is governed by the **Google Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- NanoTV is an independent application and is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google LLC.

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

NanoTV stores your configuration locally on your Android TV device:
- **Parent PIN:** Stored strictly on-device as a salted `SHA-256` cryptographic hash. Your PIN is never transmitted off your local home network.
- **Curated Shelves & Playback State:** Approved channel IDs, playlist IDs, video IDs, resume timestamps, Favorites, and Watch Later selections are stored locally on your Android TV device. You can clear watch history at any time in **Parent Settings**.

## 3. Data We Collect & Share

- **No Account Required:** NanoTV does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, or advertising identifiers (`AAID`).
- **No Third-Party Ad SDKs or Trackers:** NanoTV contains zero third-party behavioral analytics or ad-network SDKs.
- **Public Metadata Requests:** When syncing curated channels or playlists through the NanoTV API Proxy, only the public YouTube channel/playlist/video identifiers requested by the parent are processed to retrieve public video metadata.

## 4. Children's Privacy

NanoTV is intended to be installed, configured, and managed by adults (parents and guardians aged 18+) using a mandatory 4-digit Parent PIN gate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 5. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our official repository or contact the developer listed on the Google Play Store page.
